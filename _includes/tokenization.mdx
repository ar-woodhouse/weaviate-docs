Weaviate converts filter terms into tokens. The default tokenization is `word`. The `word` tokenizer splits on any non-alphanumeric character, keeps the alphanumeric characters and lowercases them. It converts a string like "Test_domain_weaviate" into "test", "domain", and "weaviate".

For details and additional tokenization methods, see [Tokenization](/weaviate/config-refs/collections#tokenization).
